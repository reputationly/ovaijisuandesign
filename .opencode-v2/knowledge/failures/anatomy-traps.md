# anatomy-traps -- body structure errors

## Activate When
Hands, feet, a face or a whole figure will be prominent in the frame, so a malformed limb or digit would spoil the subject.

## Decision Test
Ask whether the body part carries the shot. When it does (close-up, gesture, product held in hand), describe its structure explicitly. When it is only incidental, prefer a pose or crop that keeps it small or out of frame rather than piling on anatomical detail.

## Action
- Hands in focus: say how many fingers show, how they bend and what they grip, e.g. `a relaxed hand, each of the five fingers distinct, thumb wrapped around the cup`.
- Feet: state whether they are in frame and where the crop falls, e.g. `shot ends mid-calf` or `both shoes fully in frame`.
- Faces: ask for slight left-right asymmetry and skin that matches the age; photoreal portraits also read `<knowledgeDir>/failures/plastic-skin.md`.
- A photoreal or close-up face is not a reason to change model. Image work stays on the platform image route described in `<knowledgeDir>/vendors/banana.md`; if anatomy keeps failing, change pose or crop before anything else.
